# JAKESJAM — Logo & Wordmark Prompts

**Output target:** PNG, 2048×2048, transparent background.
**Use:** splash screen, lobby header, browser tab favicon (downscaled), social cards.
**House style:** see `00-prompt-conventions.md`.

The wordmark is **JAKESJAM** in a single block. Bold display typography, crystal-tech ligature flourish, faceted glow underline. The word reads at a glance even at 64px wide. Generate, then trace/clean in vector if needed (acceptable as raster for jam scale).

## Selection Notes

Pick variant 1 or 2 for the **primary mark**. Variant 5 is the **dark-on-light fallback** for documentation. Variant 6 is the **splash-screen lockup** only. Avoid variant 3 if you need the logo to remain readable below 200px wide — the filament thinning kills small-scale legibility.
