# The Order of Perpetual Respawn — the publicity bit

A parody congregation whose entire liturgical calendar is: **coffee on
Monday morning, JAKESJAM on Friday night.** Flying-Spaghetti-Monster
register — obviously a bit, warm not edgy, never punching at any real
faith. The joke is ritualizing a schedule; the marketing is that rituals
retain.

## Why it works (the sincere part under the bit)

- **Appointment content**: observances = a posting schedule people can
  set watches by. Monday post, Friday mass.
- **Identity**: "I'm in the Order" beats "I follow this channel." The
  email list already exists — it just gains a name: **the Congregation
  Roll**.
- **Vocabulary compounds**: every inside term is a comment-section
  handshake and a future shirt.

## Observances

- **Monday, morning coffee (local time)** — The Monday Bean: one chill
  post (state of the arena, week's patch notes, a blessed clip). Low
  effort, high consistency.
- **Friday, 7pm Perth** — The Opening of the Gates: server live, stream
  live, Congregation Roll gets the arena link. Mass, then scrims.

## Integration hooks (use, don't force)

- Shorts sign-off variant: "Mass is Friday. Bring your own rockets."
- Email gate copy variant: "Join the Congregation Roll — Fight Night
  invite every Friday, one Bean post on Mondays, nothing else."
- Pinned comment doctrine drops: "Blessed are the parried, for the
  rocket shall return."
- Fight Night stream: opening the gates = literal gate animation + the
  Seal breathing on the STARTING SOON scene (stream-kit already does this).
- Milestone lore: first 100 on the Roll = "the First Hundred."

## Guardrails

- Always visibly a bit. If a viewer could mistake it for sincere
  religion or for mockery of one, rewrite it.
- The bit never gates anything real: no "faithful-only" features beyond
  the email list that already exists.
- Drop any term that needs explaining twice — canon must earn itself in
  comments or die.
