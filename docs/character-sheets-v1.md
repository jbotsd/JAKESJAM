# JAKESJAM — Class Character Sheets v1

**Purpose:** portable fantasy/identity briefs, one per class, meant to be
lifted individually into another AI conversation for divergent creative
iteration — each sheet below is self-contained (copy from its `━━━` divider
to the next one). Mechanics are kept light on purpose; they're locked
separately in `docs/classes-goal.md` and `docs/card-pool-v2.md`. This doc is
for FANTASY, VOICE, and VISUAL IDENTITY — the parts still open.

**Display names LOCKED (`classes-goal.md` § Naming):**
`wizard` → **Geometrician** · `ninja` → **Interstice** · `paladin` → **Kindled** ·
`priest` → **Syzygist** (cool-white). Dev IDs stay English archetype words.

**House register (do not drift from this without a reason):** sci-fi
gnostic, not medieval fantasy. Technical-mystic, never scripture-lecture.
No robes/halos/crosses/temple imagery, no Eye-of-Providence or hexagram
compositions (an eye alone is fine; a triangle over an eye is not). Body
does not launch into the sky as an ult tax; **light** may flood vertical
space (paladin heaven-tank carve-out — self-sourced column/dome, not a
god answering). Full doctrine: `docs/visual-language-gnostic-vessel.md`.
Every sheet ends with **Open questions** — invitation to push back.

**Craft cousins:** map for *feel* and *failure modes*. **Paladin ↔ DI
Crusader** — **heaven-tank feel is the TARGET** (Jake: "heaven tank IS
the right feel though"); Autogenes source (no god/order); reject only
church costume (cross/robe/halo/temple). **Ninja ↔ DI Tempest + WoW
Rogue** — tactile dual-blade + contact economy; reject weather/Zephyrs
*and* stealth/poison/stunlock/shadow/combo HUD. **Wizard ↔ DI Wizard**
(elemental mastery: reject robe/staff and rainbow multi-elemental
spectacle — elements are universal cards; wizard identity is crystal-
geometry discipline). **Priest ↔ DI Monk** (support cousin: reject
martial-arts/melee-mobility — Ninja's lane — keep solo-viable principle
via curse/lifesteal floor). Ninja/Wizard 64px fails if DI cousin costume;
Paladin 64px **may** read heaven tank, fails only as church templar or
under-lit quiet monk. Full tables on each sheet.

## WIZARD / Geometrician — the engineer of light

**One line:** Not a caster who prays for power — an engineer who built a
weapon out of understanding the crystal's math better than anyone else who
ever picked one up.

**Anti-moodboard (Diablo Immortal Wizard):** DI's Wizard is elemental
MASTERY — "bending fire, ice, lightning, and pure arcane power" to the
caster's will, deep inter-element synergy stacking (Lightning Nova, Arcane
Torrent, Scorch, Ray of Frost), classic robe-and-staff spellcaster read,
screen-filling multi-elemental spectacle. **The load-bearing distinction:**
in this roster, elements (fire/ice/lightning) are UNIVERSAL cards any
class can draft onto any kit — they are explicitly not the wizard's
domain. So "the wizard commands fire" is simply wrong for us; a ninja or
paladin with the right draft burns just as well. What IS the wizard's
domain: geometry, charge timing, angle control — the DISCIPLINE of the
crystal, not ownership of an element. Craft worth stealing (inverted):
charge-and-release channeling (Sunlance/Overchannel are already this
shape, no elemental flavor needed, cf. Ray of Frost/Arcane Torrent).
**Hard reject:** robe/staff/spellbook costuming, "master of nature's
elements" flavor, rainbow multi-elemental screen noise — the doctrine's
"one living light, not five" rule is basically a direct rebuke of DI's
Wizard VFX habit. 64px test: if it reads arcane-robe-mage, fail.

**Psychology:** Cold curiosity, not cold heart. The wizard fights the way
a physicist argues — every bolt is a hypothesis, every fight a chance to
refine the equation. They don't hate their opponents; they're mildly
delighted when someone survives long enough to teach them something new
about the crystal. Loses interest fast in easy fights. The closest thing
this class has to a flaw: they'll take a genuinely bad trade if it answers
a question they're curious about.

**Voice:** Precise, dry, occasionally condescending without meaning to be.
Talks about combat like it's a proof. Would say "the geometry didn't
favor you" instead of "you lost."

**Combat philosophy:** Control the angle before the fight starts. Every
tool is either information (where will they be) or denial (they can't be
there). Charging a shot isn't patience, it's refusing to fire an
incomplete answer.

**Visual identity:** Crystal-shard sigil (already drawn, see
`CardDraftOverlay.ts`'s class row). Cyan-forward per the combat register
of the dual-accent doctrine — gold stays rare on this kit, earned only
through the Autogenes/house-tier cosmetic line, never the base read.
Silhouette: least armored of the four, most exposed hull-seam visible —
the wizard's confidence IS the lack of cover.

**Quote line (draft):** *"I'm not casting a spell. I'm finishing a sentence
the crystal started before either of us existed."*

**Open questions:**
- Does the wizard have a rival, or is their only real opponent the
  problem itself? (Affects whether they get a recurring NPC/bot persona.)
- Is "engineer of light" too clinical to be fun in short match banter, or
  is the dryness exactly the joke?
- Should the wizard's card-pick lines (draft-screen flavor) read as
  actual technical notes, in-character?

## NINJA / Interstice — the one who already moved

**One line:** By the time you register the ninja was there, the wave off
the second blade has already told you they aren't anymore.

**Anti-moodboards (two craft cousins — steal inverted, never costume):**

### 1) Diablo Immortal Tempest (mobility / dual-edge cousin)
Immortal's dual-blade mobility class — water/wind elementalist, "edges"
as elemental foci, **Zephyr clones that mimic attacks**, Mist-Touched,
Blade Dance / Breaker / Cascade / Crosswinds / Fox Step / Riptide /
Squall, seafaring blade-dancer lore, art-nouveau curves, storm spectacle.
**Steal inverted:** dual blades + short-range wave off the swing;
mobility as identity; decoy as a *decision copy* (Paper Double), not a
permanent elemental summon army. **Hard reject:** water/wind elemental
theming, storm VFX, seafaring costume, multi-Zephyr stage presence,
"fury of the storm" language. Not a weather mage with knives.

### 2) WoW Rogue (opener / dual-wield cousin)
Warcraft's dual-wield melee DPS — leather, **Stealth / Vanish / Sap** as
identity core, **Energy + combo points → finishers** (Eviscerate, Kidney
Shot), Cheap Shot openers, Ambush/Shadowstrike from stealth, poison
application (Assassination), shadow-dance Subtlety, Outlaw pirate swagger.
Fantasy: *the fight starts when you decide it starts; you control who
gets to act.* Specs: Assassination (poison/bleed control), Subtlety
(shadow magic, disappear-reappear burst), Outlaw (roll the bones,
between-the-eyes, swashbuckler). **Steal inverted:** dual weapons;
economy of motion; "I already moved" as the feel; brief disappear that
is a **read/reposition**, not a full stealth metagame; decoy as
commitment-lie adjacent to vanish *without* becoming untargetable
assassin. **Hard reject:**

| WoW Rogue pillar | Why it dies here |
|------------------|------------------|
| Permanent / long stealth as default identity | Arena FFA needs readable opponents; ninja is *fast and modeled you*, not invisible |
| Vanish mid-fight as "I'm gone from the game" | Our dash i-frames are evasion-in-motion, not untargetable fog |
| Poison / bleed DoT fantasy as class soul | Crystal munitions + wave; status may exist on cards, not as ninja brand |
| Combo points + finisher UI | Resonance/rotation exists globally; ninja is not an MMO resource wheel on the HUD |
| Shadow magic / shadow dance | Doctrine is sci-fi gnostic vessel, not void-assassin cosplay |
| Kidney / Cheap Shot stunlock chains | Control via position + Read tag + wave geometry, not hard-CC job |
| Leather hood / daggers-in-cloak silhouette | Vessel hull, not fantasy assassin costume |
| Outlaw pirate swagger / Subtlety edgelord | Voice stays short, present-tense, zero flair monologue |

**64px test:** fails if it reads Tempest (water/wind/clones) **or** classic
WoW rogue (leather stealth assassin / poison / vanish puff).

| Axis | DI Tempest | WoW Rogue | This ninja |
|------|------------|-----------|------------|
| Power source | Wind + water | Stealth, poison, shadow, energy | Motion + model of the opponent |
| Double | Zephyr clones (fight with you) | Vanish / mirror-ish tricks | Paper Double = input-echo **lie** |
| Open fight | Surf in, cascade | Stealth → Cheap Shot / Ambush | Already in range; wave proves it |
| Defense | Dodge through storm | Vanish, cloak, feint | Dash i-frames only — never block |
| Resource feel | Cooldowns + Zephyrs | Energy + combo points | Energy (aggression feeds rack) — no combo pip UI |
| Fantasy fail | Weather cosplay | Assassin/MMO cosplay | Crystal-tech **reader** who already left |

**Psychology:** Present-tense to the point of not really having a past
tense in conversation. Doesn't plan fights, reads them one frame at a
time and trusts the read. Not reckless — economical. Every wasted motion
is a debt paid later. The Paper Double card (a decoy sprinting your exact
last input) is basically their whole personality as a mechanic: they'd
rather leave a convincing copy of a decision than actually commit to one
early. Contrast Tempest Zephyrs (allies that multiply damage) **and**
WoW Vanish (you leave the game): Paper Double is a **lie about where you
committed**, still fully in the match, still readable as *someone*.

**Psychology, the sharper edge:** Slipstream tags a dashed-through enemy
as *Read* — the ninja's fantasy isn't stealth, weather, or poison, it's
**legibility as a weapon**: they've already modeled you, and the tag is
just them showing their work for one hit before erasing it again. WoW
rogue hides information; our ninja **weaponizes having more of it**.

**Voice:** Short. Verb-first. Doesn't narrate its own competence — the
wave does that. Closest thing to warmth is a single line acknowledging a
good dodge, immediately followed by ending the exchange anyway. Never
elemental poetry ("the tide turns") and never rogue banter ("nothing
personal," "looking for this?"). Competence is silent.

**Combat philosophy:** Distance is a lie you tell an enemy right before
you close it. The wave off the slash isn't a ranged option bolted onto
melee — it's proof the blade was already moving faster than the swing
needed to be. No elemental mastery, no stealth opener tax, no combo
finishers: the wave is **the edge still cutting air after the body
passed** — short, crystal, off a melee arc that had to land first.
Aggression feeds energy (canon) — hits restore the rack. Battery is
**contact**, not stealth.

**Tactile ability contract (Jake, 2026-07-17 — steal WoW Rogue *handfeel*,
not fantasy):** Rogue abilities feel like **knives in meat** — Mutilate,
Eviscerate, Ambush, Between the Eyes: you feel the hit before you
understand the number. Tempest abilities feel like weather. Ours must
feel like **arms, edges, and bodies**, never like a cast. Every ninja
verb is a contact event or the immediate consequence of one.

| Rule | Means in practice | Fail state |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Contact first** | Arc AABB hit, dash-through, wall kick, decoy burst at a body — something collides before the payoff | Ability works from empty air with no near-body moment |
| **Commit frames you can feel** | Swing has a short, readable commit; cancel windows are tight; whiff costs | Floaty spam with no weight; "spellcasting with knives" |
| **Hit-stop + scrape** | On-hit: brief hit-stop, blade scrape / crystal edge grit, victim micro-knock | Soft glow and damage number only |
| **Wave is aftermath, not projectile spell** | Wave spawns *from the arc that already swung* — velocity inherits swing direction; max range short enough that you were almost still in melee | Long-range wave used as a gun; wave cast without a swing |
| **Energy from contact** | Melee hit, dash-through, wall-kick restore energy — never "stood in stealth regen" | Passive regen while disengaged as the main loop |
| **Finishers are cuts, not sentences** | Undercut = one clean horizontal line through a body under 15% — Eviscerate *weight*, zero execute UI sermon | Floating skull icons, poison tick, shadow burst |
| **No hard-CC job** | Control = geometry + Read + position, not Kidney stunlock | Multi-second stun chains as class identity |
| **One body, one lie** | Paper Double is a tactile fake *runner* (legs, trail, damageable hull) | Ethereal clone army or untargetable fog |

**Ability feel targets (fantasy layer — mechanics stay in
`classes-goal.md` / `card-pool-v2.md`):**

- **Primary slash + wave:** Two-edge rhythm you can count. Each swing
  has weight; the wave is the *air still cut* — shard spray at short
  range, not a surf board. Sound: edge whistle → contact clack → wave
  hiss that dies fast.
- **Dash (signature):** Body through space. I-frames are "wasn't there,"
  not "invisible." Dash-through = you *cross their hitbox* — shoulder and
  edge, energy tick, *Read* mark like a nick on their silhouette.
- **Wall-kick:** Feet on hull, push-off you feel in the camera micro-dip
  — feeds energy because the body did work.
- **Paper Double:** A second set of **feet** on the ground, same mass
  silhouette, no muzzle light — touchable, killable. Swap is a body
  trade, not a blink cast.
- **Undercut:** When it procs, one horizontal cut with hard hit-stop —
  the clip moment. No floating execute chrome beyond a hairline only the
  ninja sees.
- **Slipstream / Read:** Dash *through* them — you feel the pass; they
  get a slash-mark nick. Next melee on *Read* hits harder because you
  already measured them, not because magic said so.

**Visual identity:** Twin-slash sigil. Smallest silhouette, fastest idle
sway. Energy-resource glow — sharper, higher-frequency pulse than wizard
cyan. **Anti-Tempest + anti-Rogue costume** (still): no water/wind
ribbons, no multi-Zephyr stage, no leather hood / cloak-dagger, no green
poison, no vanish smoke brand. **Pro-Rogue handfeel** (do this): mass in
the swing, hit-stop, scrape, body-cross dash, decoy with legs. Hull
small, edges thin, wave = crystal shard spray off a cut — not surf, not
shadow tendrils, not a cast glyph.

**Quote line (draft):** *"You're still deciding. I already left."*

**Open questions:**
- Is the ninja anyone's ally by temperament, or does duos-mode force an
  interesting friction (a class built around not committing, forced to
  commit to a partner)?
- Paper Double narrative: light-construct / input-echo / stranger —
  one-line answer. Distinct from Tempest Zephyrs **and** from WoW
  vanish (still a body someone can shoot).
- Does "Read" (Slipstream) deserve to be class-wide fantasy — live
  modeler of opponents — rather than one card's flavor? (Recommended:
  yes — this is the anti-stealth thesis.)
- Wave VFX: crystal-tech only — not Tempest surf, not Subtlety shadow.
  How much hit-stop on wave-only hits vs arc hits?
- Any card that grants true stealth / untargetable: banned for this
  chassis, or only as ultra-rare with a hard duration cap?
- How hard do we push commit frames on the primary slash before it
  fights the "already left" fantasy (weight without sluggishness)?

## PALADIN / Kindled — the one who woke up

tempering until the light was *theirs* — mass, board, and edge running
the same gold self-light — so when they hold, the arena has a sun.

enlightenment holy") — **Autogenes sourcing** (self-begotten light, no
god, no order). **FEEL correction same day (Jake: "heaven tank IS the
right feel though"):** Diablo Immortal Crusader's **heaven-tank play
feel is the target**, not the anti-moodboard. We are not de-powering the

**Craft cousin (DI Crusader) — steal FEEL, re-source metaphysics:**

Immortal Crusader is the completed heaven-tank product: fortress mass,
board as weapon, light that answers when you plant yourself, party safe
under your light (Conjuration of Light), ground that becomes *yours*

## PRIEST / Syzygist — the gift with a meter running

**One line:** Every kindness from this class comes with a clock attached,
and the honest ones are the scariest — because they never pretend
otherwise.

**Display / visual LOCKED:** **Syzygist**, **cool-white** (not violet, not
Kindled gold). Clinical gift / devotion filament.

**Anti-moodboard (Diablo Immortal Monk):** DI never shipped a pure priest
— Monk is the closest it has, and that absence is itself the useful data
point. Monk is melee martial-arts support/AoE-DPS (Seven-Sided Strike,
Cyclone Strike, Wave of Light), holy+elemental fusion, high mobility,
party buffs and absorb shields — and explicitly **not forced into a
healer role**, free to flex into pure damage. **Craft worth stealing:**
exactly that non-forced-healer principle — our Priest's curse/lifesteal
solo floor is the same instinct, arrived at independently and now
confirmed by DI's own design (a pure-healer support class doesn't survive
contact with solo queue, so nobody ships one uncompensated). **Hard
reject:** martial-arts costuming and melee-mobility identity — that's the
Ninja's lane in this roster, not this one. Our Priest reads as POSITIONED
and calm — power through entanglement (Devotion, generated by others
carrying your effects), not through closing distance and flurrying. Never
a flanking brawler-in-robes. 64px test: if it reads combat-monk, fail.

**Psychology:** Warmth with a blade folded inside it — the C4 tone
target for this class is explicitly "unsettling-benevolent," and
Borrowed Time (heals 30, drains 15 back unless the target keeps fighting)
IS that psychology as a mechanic: the priest doesn't believe in gifts
that don't ask something back, and they're right often enough that
nobody argues. Not evil. Transactional in a way that happens to keep
people alive, which is either the same thing as caring or close enough
that the priest has stopped worrying about the difference.

**Singular viability (locked):** Syzygist is **win-capable alone** — FFA,
solo queue, practice. Not a duos passenger. Teams = peak; singular = floor
and must be real. Solo is a **unique problematic encounter** space (enemy
entanglement, Contagion chains, self-ward weakness as a read) — not
“duos with fake friends.”

**Psychology, the FFA-solo edge:** Same transactional mind, colder
target. No ally → meter on **enemies** (curses, lifesteal, Contagion).
Solo Syzygist *takes*; duo Syzygist *lends*. Both are honest.

**Voice:** Gentle cadence, transactional content. Would say "you'll want
to keep moving" (a mechanical truth about Borrowed Time's drain
condition, delivered as if it were simply advice) instead of "the healing
fades if you stop fighting." Solo kill line colder: "You were enough."

**Combat philosophy:** Devotion comes from **others carrying your
effects** — ally *or* enemy: buffs, heals, curses, wards, DoTs. Fantasy
is entanglement, not "must have a friend." Alone you still bind people
(curses); with a duo you bind both sides. Peak power with allies; **floor
power alone is intentional and tuned**. Catalog must support a singular
3-slot spec (offense/single/defense paths), not only buff stacks.

**Visual identity:** Radiant-ring sigil. **Cool-white** locked — clinical
gift / devotion filament; not Kindled gold, not void violet. Contagion
and Flock tells must be READABLE from outside — threat is otherwise
invisible (no big blade, no big bolt).

**Quote line (draft):** *"I already gave you more than you'll pay back.
Try not to waste it."*

**Open questions:**
- "Dark cleric" was the shorthand used when this class was speced — does
  that read as too villain-coded for a class players are meant to WANT
  to main, or is the edge exactly the appeal?
- What does a priest's victory line sound like when THEY land the kill
  vs. when an ally they buffed does? (Devotion's whole fantasy is other
  people's success being partly theirs — worth writing both.)
- Should wards-on-self read as a deliberate weakness the priest resents,
  or as a belief system (they genuinely think protecting others matters
  more than protecting themselves, no metaphysics required)?
- Color slot: violet (reserved "future void/defense" in the visual
  doctrine) vs. a new cool-white — needs a decision before any art work
  starts on this class.

## How to use these with another AI

Copy one class's block (from its `━━━` divider through its **Open
questions**) into a fresh conversation with whatever model you're pairing
with. Good framing for that handoff: *"This is a character sheet for a
class in a sci-fi-gnostic arena shooter. Push on the fantasy, voice, and
open questions — don't touch the mechanics, they're locked elsewhere."*
Bring back whatever lands; nothing here is final until it's folded back
into `classes-goal.md`.
