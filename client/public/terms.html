JAKESJAM is a free-to-play, browser-based multiplayer arena shooter, made and operated by Jake Colson, trading as Intrepid Development, based in Perth, Western Australia ("we", "us"). It's under active development. Features can change, break, get added, or get removed. Match history and world state can be wiped. The whole thing can go offline for maintenance without notice.

Who can play

Anyone can open and play JAKESJAM. If you're under 13, you can play the full game, matches and cosmetics included, without giving us an email address — the signup prompt is optional. If you're under 18, get a parent or guardian's OK before making a real-money purchase (see Purchases). Making a purchase requires you to be old enough, or otherwise authorized, to use the payment method — Stripe enforces that on their end.

Your callsign & player ID

JAKESJAM doesn't use accounts, passwords, or logins. Your browser generates a random anonymous player ID and stores it locally, alongside whatever callsign you typed in. That's how the game recognizes you across a session and how purchased cosmetics stay attached to you. Clearing your browser data or switching devices makes you a new player — anything tied only to the old local ID, including cosmetic ownership, may not carry over.

Callsigns are filtered for disallowed characters but not reviewed by a human before you play. Don't use one to impersonate or harass another player — see Acceptable use.
